# Street visual review

Review basis: the [current visual gallery](VISUALS.md), especially the 667 × 375 guest street and the all-level neighbour sheet. This is a visual direction for the next curated-art pass, not evidence from a physical phone or a signed testnet session.

## What reads well now

- The five building types already have different rooflines and footprints, and the two variants within each type use different structures.
- Level changes are visible in the neighbour sheet without crossing the height limits or covering curb spaces.
- The logo circle, rule sign, parking stalls and main action are consistently placed in the street scene.

## What needs improvement

1. **Material identity at gameplay scale.** Most facades share the same pale walls, blue windows and dark outline. At 667 × 375, the roofline differences survive but material differences weaken. Give each type a dominant surface and detail language: brick and display glass for retail, timber or greenhouse ribs for cafes, enamel and marquee lighting for restaurants, framed glazing or concrete bands for offices, and warm plaster with balconies or planting for homes. Keep the four player palette choices as accents, so a palette change does not erase the type.
2. **Stronger variant separation.** The second variant should change the massing and entrance placement, not only decoration. Compare both variants of the same type at levels 1 and 4 in a real street row before accepting revised assets. Preserve the existing 140px width, level height caps and approved logo/billboard rectangles.
3. **Upgrade character.** Higher levels currently grow taller; add one or two visible features per level that match the type, such as an extra shop bay, greenhouse roof, diner terrace, office skybridge detail or home balcony. Keep level 4 recognizable as the same player's building.
4. **Approved advertising in context.** The guest street screenshots show placeholder initials and empty ad space. Capture a current game street with an approved logo and billboard, its owner name and its curb rule, then review whether the ad remains legible beside intersections and other buildings. The operator must approve the exact media revision before this capture is used as publication evidence.
5. **Street depth and focus.** The phone scene has a large uniform sky and a light background row. Add restrained foreground details and varied background silhouettes only after the building surfaces read clearly; the player's car, two curb stalls and rule sign must remain easy to find.

## Acceptance for the next art pass

- At 667 × 375, a reviewer can identify the five building types and distinguish both variants without reading their labels.
- Each level remains within the source-pixel envelope in [ART_SPEC](ART_SPEC.md); logo, billboard and name placements remain valid after any shape change.
- One phone street capture, one desktop street capture and the full neighbour sheet show the revised art. A physical-phone review is still required for the Phase-2 gate.
- The revision stays curated-only. Paid prompt generation remains disabled until the separate provider test is authorized.
