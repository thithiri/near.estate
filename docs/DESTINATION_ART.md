The generated assets were made with the built-in image generation tool, then encoded as WebP for the game. Their original transparent PNGs remain under `art/source/destination-art`. The horse runner sheet has four equal cells in the order chestnut/coral, dark bay/blue, palomino/gold, charcoal/green. The practice car sheet has four equal cells in the order red, blue, yellow, green. Both activity renderers load these images asynchronously and retain their code-drawn fallback. Race timing, winners, practice checkpoints and rewards are independent of image loading.

## Final generation prompts

### Horse runners

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent 2D game sprite sheet for near.estate's animated horse race canvas. Create FOUR separate full-body side-view racehorses with small jockeys, all facing RIGHT, arranged in a single horizontal row with generous empty transparent gaps and no overlap. Horse 1 chestnut coat with coral jockey silks; horse 2 dark bay coat with sky-blue silks; horse 3 palomino coat with golden-yellow silks; horse 4 charcoal coat with mint-green silks. All four horses have anatomically recognizable horse heads, necks, manes, four galloping legs and tails; riders seated in racing posture. Match the existing Harbor Arcade game art: polished friendly 2D illustrated game sprites, deep navy ink contours, warm cream highlights, crisp colorful shapes, front/side elevation, detailed enough to read at small size. Every horse occupies its own equal-width quarter and has the same baseline, silhouette scale, and direction. Genuinely transparent background. No track, shadows, text, numbers, logos, frames, watermark, extra horses or cropped limbs.

### Horse track

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: background plate for near.estate's animated horse race, wide 16:9 game canvas. Draw a polished, friendly, front-on 2D illustrated harbor horse racetrack in the same Harbor Arcade style as an ornate teal-and-cream horse-course gate: deep navy ink outlines, teal grandstand roof and railings, warm cream stone, coral and gold flags, tiny colorful spectator silhouettes, bright blue sky. Composition is SIDE VIEW, not perspective: the upper 35 percent is grandstand and sky; the lower 65 percent is FOUR clearly separated, perfectly HORIZONTAL brown racing lanes stretching edge-to-edge, with thin cream lane dividers. Keep the lane region open and unobstructed for horse sprites and a finish line added later by code. No horses, no people on the track, no numbers, no words, no logo, no finish line, no UI, no border, no watermark. Crisp illustrated game art with hand-painted detail; avoid flat geometric placeholder shapes.

### Practice track

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: top-down background plate for near.estate's playable practice course canvas. Portrait 3:4 composition, strictly orthographic bird's-eye view, no perspective. A perfectly STRAIGHT vertical asphalt practice road from the top edge to bottom edge, centered and occupying about 70% of the image width; lush planted verge, cream-and-coral safety kerbs and tidy stacked navy tires along both sides. Harbor Arcade 2D game illustration style: deep navy ink contours, teal plants, warm cream and coral accents, polished texture, crisp readable shapes. Leave the central asphalt OPEN and unobstructed for three driving lanes, checkpoint lines, cones and a moving car that will be drawn separately by code. Keep the width of the road constant from top to bottom. No cars, cones, letters, numbers, arrows, logos, finish line, lane markings, text, UI, frame or watermark.

### Practice cars

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent top-down car sprite sheet for near.estate's three-lane practice course. Four isolated small hatchback race cars viewed STRICTLY FROM ABOVE, noses pointed UP, arranged in one horizontal row with equal-size cells and generous transparent gaps. Same body silhouette and scale in each cell; colors left to right red/coral, blue/cyan, yellow/gold, green/mint. Deep navy ink outlines, cream highlights, bright windshield and rear glass, readable wheels and headlights, polished friendly Harbor Arcade 2D game art. Full cars visible, centered, no perspective or side view. Genuinely transparent background. No shadows, track, cones, text, numbers, logos, border, watermark, extra cars or cropped parts.
