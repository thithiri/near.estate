# Hearts and fuel: first playable loop

One parking session represents a city trip. The existing car durability field is shown as fuel; arrow-key movement does not consume it because driving position is not on-chain. The game-owned Central Garage presents itself as **NEAR Fuel & Service**, with a one-tap refill. Both the pump and car screen use the existing `repair(car_id, points)` contract action. The internal field and method names remain for state compatibility. A separate racing-damage stat can be added only if racing later needs one.

The guest pilot and shared testnet use 100 fuel, 10 fuel per park and 0.2 coin per fuel point. A full tank therefore lasts ten parking sessions and the refill cost is 2 coins per session, equal to the previous cost of 2 durability at 1 coin each. An empty car can still travel to the station; the existing emergency repair action can supply one trip when no car can park and the player cannot afford a refill.

Entertainment destinations are Cinema, Burger Stop, Practice Course and Horse Course. After an activity finishes, the client offers a heart check-in. The contract verifies that the caller's car is legally parked on the same road and curb side within two plot positions of the site, that the session is still active, and that this destination has not been claimed today. It cannot prove that a film was watched or a race was finished; the client triggers the call at activity completion. The small, bounded payout limits the incentive to skip the activity.

The default heart policy allows two distinct check-ins per account per UTC day, two held hearts per car, and two bonuses paid per account per UTC day. A legal `collect` after at least 10 minutes consumes one heart and credits 2 extra internal coins. A short collection leaves the heart ready for a later trip. Ticketed or expired sessions do not consume it. Hearts persist across days until used. The bonus is a separate `visit_bonus_paid` event, so ordinary settlement math and plot-owner meter income remain unchanged. At default settings, at most 4 bonus coins can be minted for an account in one UTC day.

The heart policy lives in a bounded, admin-settable sidecar config to preserve the serialized version-2 city root. Per-account heart state is also bounded and funded by the game contract. The existing parking-agent key cannot claim hearts or spend coins on fuel; the human owner still refills that car.

## Verified release

The [imported-state rehearsal](../deployments/runtime-upgrade-sandbox-f32cff1a48532d3c.json), [guarded code release](../deployments/runtime-upgrade-live-f32cff1a48532d3c.json) and [finalized postcheck](../deployments/runtime-upgrade-postcheck-f32cff1a48532d3c.json) preserved the 100-plot city and reopened it. A signed [testnet journey](../deployments/testnet-heart-fuel-journey-2026-11-14.json) then advanced config version 2 to 3 with only `park_durability_cost=10` and `repair_cost_per_point=200000`, parked a disposable car at Market North, checked in at Near & Far Burgers, collected 3.183333 coins including a separately emitted 2-coin heart bonus, and refilled 10 fuel for 2 coins. The heart minimum was shortened to 60 seconds only during this test and restored to 600 seconds afterward. Existing parking sessions retained their snapshotted earning terms. The browser guest journey passed at desktop and two phone sizes; a connected wallet journey in the browser remains to be checked.
